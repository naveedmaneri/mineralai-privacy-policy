MineralAI Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

1. Introduction

MineralAI is an AI-powered mineral identification application. It helps users identify mineral specimens from photographs and provides information about their properties and characteristics.

2. Information We Process

MineralAI processes photographs selected from your device gallery or captured using your camera for mineral identification.

The application does not currently require users to create an account or provide personal registration information.

3. Camera and Photo Gallery

Camera permission is requested when you choose to photograph a mineral specimen. Photo library access is requested when you choose an existing image. These permissions are used to obtain images for mineral identification.

4. Image Processing and Third-Party Services

When you submit a photograph for identification, the image is transmitted over the internet to our mineral identification service hosted on Hugging Face. The service uses a machine learning model to generate mineral identification predictions.

Hugging Face operates the infrastructure hosting this service and may process technical information under its applicable policies. Images and related requests may be processed or logged by the hosting provider. Please consult Hugging Face's privacy documentation for further information.

5. Data Storage and Retention

MineralAI does not currently provide user accounts or a cloud-based personal photo library. Photographs are transmitted to the identification service for processing. Their handling and retention by the hosting provider are subject to its applicable policies.

6. Advertising and Payments

The current version of MineralAI does not offer integrated advertising or paid subscriptions. If these features are introduced, this policy will be updated accordingly.

7. Children's Privacy

MineralAI is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. The application does not currently provide account registration.

8. Data Security

We use HTTPS when transmitting photographs to our identification service. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when application functionality, services or applicable requirements change. Updated versions will be published at this address.
